# IncomeDesk Launch Campaign — 15 Social Posts

Platform-neutral drafts for the `#IncomeDeskLaunch` campaign. Each post includes caption copy, suggested on-art text, and a visual brief that can be used to generate a separate image. The sequence moves from the freelancer finance problem to product introduction, feature education, and an invite-only beta call to action.

## Creative system

- **Brand palette:** IncomeDesk green `#08764A`, deep slate `#0F172A`, white, and cool light gray. Use the existing IncomeDesk logo asset when composing final artwork.
- **Look:** Calm, modern financial workspace; clean layouts, generous negative space, approachable freelance context. Mix product-led abstract dashboard visuals with a few human work scenes.
- **Format:** Square social post, 1:1. Keep key content centered with safe margins so crops can be adapted later.
- **Text:** Generate clean artwork with room for copy, then place the exact headline and logo in layout software. Avoid relying on generated UI text, numbers, or tiny labels.
- **Accuracy:** Show a fictional/de-identified dashboard only. Do not imply bank syncing, payment processing, tax filing, guaranteed savings, or beta results that have not happened.
- **CTA:** Replace `[beta link]` with the real signup URL only after access is open. Until then, use “Follow for beta updates.”
