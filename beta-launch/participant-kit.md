## First-session guide

1. Create your account at **[beta URL]** and use a password you do not use elsewhere.
2. Set up only the profile and account details needed for the workflows you want to try. You may use fictional or de-identified entries if you prefer.
3. Try one task: **[choose a confirmed core task, such as record an income entry and review the dashboard]**.
4. Note anything confusing, unexpected, slow, or incorrect. Do not include actual amounts or account details in a report unless the support owner provides a secure, approved process.
5. Send feedback through **[support channel]**. Include the page, what you expected, what happened, approximate time, and browser/device. Redact names, amounts, and account references from screenshots.

## What we would like you to try

Choose two or three tasks available in the current beta:

- Set up an account and find where to review your balance.
- Record an income or expense and explain how it changes the dashboard.
- Create or review a budget.
- Create an invoice and, if enabled, record a payment and check the remaining balance.
- Review a project, report, subscription, or tax estimate that is relevant to your work.

You do not need to complete every task. Tell us which tasks match your current process and which do not.
