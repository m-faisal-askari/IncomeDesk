# Beta Metrics and Decision Scorecard

Use counts plus interviews and issue evidence. A small cohort cannot establish broad market demand or prove that a workflow is safe in every case. The targets below are starting hypotheses; change them before invitations if they do not fit the beta's scope or support capacity.

## Definitions

- **Invited:** A person who received a beta invitation.
- **Joined:** An invited person who completed signup and accepted the beta terms/notice where applicable.
- **Activated:** A joined tester who creates or selects an account, records at least one income or expense, and views the resulting dashboard within 72 hours. If the beta flow differs, define the event before Day 1 and keep it stable.
- **Meaningful weekly use:** A tester returns in a later week and completes a core finance action, not merely a login.
- **Feedback report:** One distinct issue or product observation. Deduplicate repeats while preserving affected-user count.
- **Critical issue:** Any S0 or S1 issue from the [operations guide](feedback-operations.md).

Track event counts and workflow completion only. Do not put transaction descriptions, amounts, invoice details, or account balances into product analytics.

## Suggested targets for the first cohort

| Measure | Starting target | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Invited cohort | 15–20 people maximum | Limit is based on support capacity, not a growth quota. |
| Activation | At least 60% of joiners within 72 hours | If lower, investigate invitation fit and first-use friction before expanding. |
| Task completion | At least 4 of 5 observed testers complete a chosen core task without facilitator intervention | Use observed sessions and note where help was needed. |
| Follow-up evidence | At least 5 interviews or structured follow-ups | Include active, inactive, and blocked testers where possible. |
| Critical unresolved issues | 0 at each expansion and closeout gate | Any unresolved S0/S1 blocks expansion. |
| Repeat use | At least 40% of activated testers return in a later week | Directional only for this small, short beta. Ask what caused return or drop-off. |
