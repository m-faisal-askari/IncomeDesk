# Brag Plan: IncomeDesk

## What is this app?
IncomeDesk is a financial operations workspace for independent contractors and self-employed professionals, bringing invoices, income, expenses, project profitability, tax reserves, and budgets into one ledger.

## The angle
Freelance income has a gap between sending an invoice and actually receiving the money. Show that distinction first, then follow a payment into confirmed income, a clear cash-flow view, and a planned tax reserve. The whole story is a freelancer's money moving through the product, not a generic collection of finance features.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An invoice marked **OUTSTANDING** fills the vertical frame. The headline lands: **“Invoice sent ≠ cash received.”** The visual makes the product's core accounting distinction instantly understandable.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Tap **Record Payment**; the sample invoice changes to paid and a confirmed income row appears in the ledger.
- Three dashboard figures arrive in order: **Net Cash Received**, **Total Expenses**, then the resulting **Net Cash Flow**.
- The last dashboard beat connects that money picture to **Estimated Tax Reserve**, shown as a planning estimate.

## Outro / punchline
The reserve card settles into a calm final brand frame: **IncomeDesk — Designed for independent freelancers.**

## User flow worth showing
Create/send an invoice → record its payment settlement → see confirmed income and updated cash flow → review the estimated tax reserve. The invoice is not counted as received income until payment is recorded.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm, confident mobile-first money clarity for freelancers
- Interpretation: a composed product film with clear pauses, restrained movement, legible financial figures, and a warm, light audio bed.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Share copy (draft)
IncomeDesk brings freelancer invoices, cash flow, expenses, and estimated tax reserves into one clear financial workspace.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, polished support with quiet interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (bundled, steady and clean)
- Music treatment: begin at 0s around 0.30 volume; short fade-in, remain beneath the UI, and fade out over the last second.
- Music cue guidance: bundled vol. 12 preset, estimated 109.96 BPM. Aim major reveals at 8.74s (payment confirmation) and 17.47s (tax reserve); space dashboard rows at 10.37s, 11.46s, and 12.55s, holding each after its reveal.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the existing IncomeDesk green halo follow the measured music envelope by a few percentage points. No waveform, equalizer, or pulsing text.
- SFX posture: sparse and low risk; a quiet button click, one payment confirmation, and one soft final reserve/brand accent.
- Audio-coupled moments: simulated **Record Payment** click; payment confirmation; the dashboard row sequence; tax reserve landing.
- Restraint rule: keep the music under the financial information, with no sharp repeated ticks or voiceover.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The invoice gap — 4.4s
Open on a clearly labeled **DEMO DATA** invoice card with `INV-104`, `OUTSTANDING`, and `$2,500.00`. Land the headline **“Invoice sent ≠ cash received.”** Keep the invoice visually dominant; use the real IncomeDesk logo only in the ending scene.
Sequential/interaction: yes — headline settles first, then the sample invoice card fields arrive as one grouped UI reveal. Hold the headline long enough to read.
Audio intent: calm opening, one subtle card reveal.
Audio-coupled idea: soft reveal accent; no typing or narration.
Music: steady clean bed begins quietly.
Transition mood: an invoice action carries the next view in from the right → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Payment becomes income — 5.2s
Show **Record Payment** being tapped on the demo invoice. Resolve the status to **PAID**, show the actual confirmation copy **“Payment recorded.”**, then place a ledger row **INCOME +$2,500.00**. A small caption can clarify **“Confirmed income”**. Values are fictional sample figures, not customer data.
Sequential/interaction: yes — button tap → paid status → ledger income row, in that order.
Audio intent: quiet interaction click, then a soft confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: click at the tap, confirmation accent at 8.74s.
Music: continue beneath the interface.
Transition mood: the ledger view pushes upward into the dashboard → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Cash flow at a glance — 5.1s
Recreate the real **Financial Overview** dashboard in a portrait-friendly layout. Keep a visible **DEMO DATA** chip. Reveal these fictional sample figures in order: `Net Cash Received  $2,500`, `Total Expenses  $740`, `Net Cash Flow  $1,760`. The figures reconcile and are illustrative only.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three metric rows appear at about 10.37s, 11.46s, and 12.55s, with at least 0.8s settled reading time per row; hold all three together afterward.
Audio intent: measured rhythm; the final net-cash-flow card receives the strongest emphasis.
Audio-coupled idea: small row arrivals follow alternate beats; emphasize the final total without speeding the text.
Music: steady and restrained.
Transition mood: a reserve panel slides in from below as the next step in the same financial flow → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Plan the reserve — 6.3s
Show **Estimated Tax Reserve** as a clearly labeled planning estimate: `$352` on a fictional `$1,760` sample profit at a demo configured reserve target of `20%`. Include the source UI's **Planning Estimates Only** wording in smaller readable text. Finish on the IncomeDesk logo and **“Designed for independent freelancers.”** Hold the final brand line for at least 2s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — reserve figure resolves first; logo and final line arrive last and remain settled.
Audio intent: reassuring resolution, one soft brand payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: reserve reveal near 17.47s; let the last line remain still while music fades.
Music: warm bed fades gently at the end.
Transition mood: settle to the IncomeDesk end frame.

**Music mood for this video:** polished, warm, steady.
**Audio summary:** a quiet modern business bed supports a payment click, a confirmed-income reveal, and a restrained reserve/brand landing.

## Fictional data note
All invoice numbers and dollar figures in the video are invented examples created for the composition. No real customer, account, invoice, or personal data is used.
