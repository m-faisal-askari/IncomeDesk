# IncomeDesk: production finances in frame

## Audience and story
Independent filmmakers, editors, DPs, and small production teams. Follow the fictional short film Afterlight from wrap to project profitability. The slate is only a filmmaking cue; IncomeDesk handles the financial side of freelance projects.

## Visual system
- Portrait canvas: 1080 x 1920
- Background: #000000 and #0E0E11; raised cards: #111115
- Text: #FFFFFF and #D1D5DB; muted labels: #9CA3AF
- IncomeDesk accent: #35C98A, with #08764A for darker fills
- Font: Inter, with tabular figures
- Safe edges: 72px

## Scenes
1. Hook: "The shoot wrapped. Did the project pay?" A small production slate introduces Afterlight.
2. Project Financials: $18,000 contract value and revenue realized, $12,600 direct project costs, $5,400 take-home profit, and 30% margin.
3. Production Budget: $13,500 planned, $12,600 spent (93%), and $900 remaining. Cost examples: camera and lens rental $4,800; contractor crew $5,200; sound and location permits $2,600.
4. Close: "Keep production finances in frame." Name the IncomeDesk Project Management & Production Financials view.

## Accuracy and craft
- All example figures and the Afterlight project are fictional demo data.
- Project costs sum to $12,600; $18,000 less costs equals $5,400; $12,600 of a $13,500 budget rounds to 93%.
- Budget remaining and take-home profit are separate measures.
- Keep the UI cards crisp, labels readable at portrait scale, and motion restrained.
- Do not suggest that IncomeDesk edits footage, creates call sheets, or schedules crew.
