# Brag Plan: IncomeDesk

## What is this app?
IncomeDesk is a financial workspace for independent freelancers to track confirmed income, expenses, cash flow, and estimated tax reserves.

## The angle
Make this a short worked example, not another “where did it go?” story. Follow one fictional $6,200 client payment through business costs, net cash flow, and a tax-planning estimate. The point is that a payment is the first number in a freelancer's financial picture, not the whole picture.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“A client pays $6,200.” Land the thought: “But that's only the first number.” Show the confirmed payment as the first step in a clear numerical walkthrough.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Show three realistic, fictional business costs adding to $2,860: workspace rent $1,150, tools and software $980, project expenses $730.
- Recreate IncomeDesk's Financial Overview: $6,200 net cash received, $2,860 total expenses, and $3,340 net cash flow.
- Turn the same sample cash-flow figure into a clearly qualified estimated reserve: 20% demo target on $3,340 = $668.

## Outro / punchline
“IncomeDesk. See the whole picture.” Keep the IncomeDesk wordmark beside the product's estimated-reserve UI and disclaimer.

## User flow worth showing
Record a settled client payment → see the work's expenses and resulting net cash flow in Financial Overview → plan an estimated tax reserve using a configured demo target.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: a crisp financial explainer built around one payment and the numbers that follow it
- Interpretation: use simple subtraction, measured movement, and long enough holds to understand each number; keep the tax figure visibly labeled as an estimate.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#000000` and `#0E0E11`
- Accent: `#35C98A`, with brand green `#08764A`
- Text: `#FFFFFF`, secondary `#D1D5DB`, muted `#9CA3AF`
- Display font: Inter
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the Financial Overview with cash received, expenses, and net cash flow.

## Share copy (draft)
A client payment is only the first number. IncomeDesk brings freelance income, expenses, cash flow, and tax planning into one clear view.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, steady bed for a confident financial explainer
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` by Sascha Ende / ende.app, CC BY 4.0
- Music treatment: start at a moderate low level, keep narration off, and fade gently under the final brand frame.
- Music cue guidance: bundled Vol. 12 preset at about 109.96 BPM; use the 8.74s strong cue for the Financial Overview and the 17.47s cue for the estimated reserve. Beat-grid timing may support expense rows if their full set stays readable.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; existing brand glows breathe with the music, without waveform or visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft, and motion-matched; light payment confirmation, small cost-row accents, one soft reserve cue.
- Audio-coupled moments: confirmed payment card, expense total, cash-flow result, reserve amount.
- Restraint rule: do not make the math feel like investment or tax advice; the figures are a fictional planning example.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — First number — 5.0s
Hook: “A client pays $6,200.” Follow-up: “But that's only the first number.” Recreate the app's recorded-payment state with a single $6,200 confirmed-income card. Label it demo data. Show an actual settled payment, not a draft invoice.
Sequential/interaction: yes — payment confirmation settles first; the number then holds.
Audio intent: quiet payment tap and warm confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: card reveal lands on the early beat and remains visible.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Count the costs — 5.3s
Copy: “Your work has costs, too.” Show three demo expense rows (workspace rent −$1,150, tools and software −$980, project expenses −$730) adding to total expenses −$2,860. Keep each row large; reveal the rows quickly, then hold the complete list and total.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three expense rows appear in order, then their total resolves beneath them.
Audio intent: restrained ticks for the cost rows; soft emphasis on the total.
Audio-coupled idea: accent the first and final row, not every line.
Transition mood: smooth upward slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Read the cash flow — 5.96s
Show the IncomeDesk Financial Overview with sample values: Net Cash Received $6,200, Total Expenses $2,860, Net Cash Flow $3,340. Copy: “Now the monthly picture makes sense.” Use the actual dashboard labels and state that figures are samples.
Sequential/interaction: yes — reveal the three dashboard rows with readable spacing and keep the full overview on screen.
Audio intent: measured pulse, with a soft accent as net cash flow resolves.
Audio-coupled idea: begin the overview transition on the 8.74s strong cue; let the dashboard rows settle at their natural readable pace.
Transition mood: clean upward slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Plan a reserve — 6.3s
Show Estimated Tax Reserve $668, Demo target 20%, Sample profit $3,340, and “Planning Estimates Only.” Make clear this is a sample planning estimate based on fictional figures and a configured rate. Close on the IncomeDesk icon and wordmark with “See the whole picture.”
Sequential/interaction: yes — reveal the reserve amount after its context, then settle the brand lockup.
Audio intent: one soft confirmation cue on the estimate, then a gentle fade.
Audio-coupled idea: land the $668 estimate on the 17.47s cue and hold the closing brand.
Transition mood: soft resolve → end
